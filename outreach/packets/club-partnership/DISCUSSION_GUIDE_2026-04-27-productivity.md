# Discussion guide — “Has generative AI already raised economy-wide productivity?”

**Brief URL:** https://kaushikatla-cell.github.io/neural-report/briefs/2026-04-27-has-gen-ai-raised-productivity.html  
**Run time:** 25–35 minutes · **Audience:** HS / early college · **Faculty sponsor:** recommended

## Learning objectives

- Distinguish **macro productivity** from **firm-level studies**.
- Practice reading a primary source link (IMF / BLS / NBER) without over-claiming.

## Opening (3 min)

Ask: *“If AI is huge, shouldn’t GDP per hour jump right away?”* Collect quick answers; don’t resolve yet.

## Section A — map the claims (10 min)

In pairs, list **three** headlines students have seen about AI + jobs/productivity.  
Then, against the brief’s TL;DR, label each headline **supported / overstated / unclear**.

## Section B — evidence triage (12 min)

Pick **one** source from the brief (IMF note, BLS page, or NBER paper).  
Each group answers:

1. What is the **population** the evidence covers (country, sector, workers)?
2. What is the **most modest** accurate claim you can defend from that source alone?

## Section C — “what would change our mind?” (8 min)

Use the brief’s falsifiers. Debate: **what new data** would flip your mind from “too early to tell” to “likely big effect”?

## Exit ticket (2 min)

One sentence: **What is one thing you will check next time you read an AI productivity headline?**

## Optional homework

Update one slide for your club newsletter linking **methodology** + one brief URL.
