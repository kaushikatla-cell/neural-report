# Validation playbook — Neural Report

Goal: **two or more** independent signals that NRP is used, cited, or partnered outside your own accounts.

## Founding team (use in intros)

| Name | Default lane when pitching |
|------|----------------------------|
| **Divyam Gupta** | Editorial lead — accuracy, topic fit, publish |
| **Kaushik Atla** | Research & web — sources, site, analytics |
| **Shivam Pande** | Design & partnerships — visuals, clubs, guests |

**Tip:** Lead with the full team once (“co-founded by Divyam Gupta, Kaushik Atla, and Shivam Pande”), then sign in your own voice. For partnership first contact, **Shivam** is the default DRI unless the thread is technical (then **Kaushik**).

## Artifact list (keep files/screenshots in `outreach/artifacts/`)

Create a folder `outreach/artifacts/` locally (gitignored if sensitive) or store exports with PII redacted:

| Artifact | Example |
|----------|---------|
| Syllabus PDF or email | Teacher says “use NRP brief for discussion week 4” |
| Club newsletter / slide deck | Econ club links your site |
| Guest confirmation | Calendar invite + published Q&A |
| Press / blog quote | Outlet links your methodology or a brief |
| MOU or simple LOI | One-pager with school org (non-lawyer template below) |

## 1. High school econ / debate / CS club partnership

### Email template (cold, student org president + faculty sponsor CC)

**Subject:** Free AI + econ “Evidence Brief” for [Club name] — sourced, classroom-safe

Hi [Recipient name],

Neural Report is co-founded by **Divyam Gupta**, **Kaushik Atla**, and **Shivam Pande** (neural.report / @neural.report). I’m **[YOUR FIRST NAME]** — I focus on **[editorial | research & web | design & partnerships]** for the project.

We publish weekly **NRP Evidence Briefs**: short explainers at the intersection of AI and economics, with primary sources and an explicit corrections policy.

We’d love to offer [Club] a **one-page discussion guide** for any brief you pick from our archive—or co-host a **20-minute Zoom** walkthrough of one topic for your members. No cost; we’re building rigorous public explainers.

If you’re open, which week works for a 15-minute call? We can include a second co-founder if helpful (Divyam, Kaushik, or Shivam).

Thanks,  
**[YOUR FULL NAME]** · [email] · [phone optional]

Ready-made sign-offs (pick one and add email):

- Thanks, **Shivam Pande** (partnerships / design)
- Thanks, **Kaushik Atla** (research / site)
- Thanks, **Divyam Gupta** (editorial)

### Follow-up (7 days)

**Subject:** Re: Neural Report × [Club]

Hi [Name], bumping this in case it got buried. Happy to send our methodology page if your sponsor wants to review sourcing standards first: [URL]/methodology.html

## 5. If someone says no
