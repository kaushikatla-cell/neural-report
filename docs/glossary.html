Glossary

A practical glossary of terms we use frequently in AI + economics briefs.

General-purpose technology (GPT)

A technology with broad applicability that can transform many sectors over long diffusion cycles.

Labor productivity

Output per hour worked, usually measured by statistical agencies like BLS with specific scope definitions.

Total factor productivity (TFP)

Residual productivity measure after accounting for labor and capital inputs; sensitive to model assumptions.

Maximum employment

Fed mandate concept that is broad and not fixed to one unemployment number across all contexts.

Inference

The stage where trained models generate outputs in production; often the dominant operational cost center.

Fine-tuning

Further training a base model on narrower data/tasks; can improve task performance with added trade-offs.

Exposure vs. adoption

Exposure means tasks could be affected; adoption means organizations actually deploy and redesign workflows.

Counterfactual

The “what would have happened otherwise” baseline required for causal claims.
