Signature series roadmap

12-week arcs designed for depth, not headline churn.

Current flagship: AI productivity measurement

  • Week 1-2: baseline definitions (BLS, OECD)
  • Week 3-5: micro-evidence on workplace effects
  • Week 6-8: macro transmission and policy channels
  • Week 9-12: uncertainty, falsifiers, and synthesis brief

Arc A: AI + labor market

Task exposure, adoption frictions, wage dispersion, and transition policy.

Arc B: AI + infrastructure

Compute supply chain, electricity demand, grid constraints, and permitting bottlenecks.

Arc C: AI + policy

Industrial policy, monetary policy framing, and competition/governance institutions.

Detailed planning docs live in the repository: 12-week outline and weeks 5-12 drafts.
