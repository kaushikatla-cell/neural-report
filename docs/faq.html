FAQ

How NRP works, what our evidence standard is, and how to evaluate our work.

What is an NRP Evidence Brief?

A weekly, source-first explainer that starts with a concrete question, lays out claims and evidence, and ends with explicit uncertainty and falsifiers.

How do you decide what counts as evidence?

We prioritize primary sources (agencies, datasets, filings, original papers), then clearly label secondary synthesis. Details are on the Methodology page.

Do you use AI tools to write?

We may use assistive drafting, but factual claims are human-verified against listed sources before publish. Hallucinated citations are never accepted as evidence.

How do you handle mistakes?

Material errors are logged in Corrections and linked from affected briefs. Typo-only fixes may be silent if meaning is unchanged.

Where can I see your operating metrics?

Public operational signals are on Dashboard; monthly narrative retros are on Transparency.

Can I use NRP briefs in class or clubs?

Yes. Cite the brief URL and date, and encourage students to inspect original sources. For outreach and packets, see the repository outreach folder.

How can experts or organizations collaborate?

We support guest quotes, short Q&As, syllabus/resource inclusion, and club workshops. Use Subscribe or GitHub issues to start a thread.
