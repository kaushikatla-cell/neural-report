Methodology

How we choose topics, handle uncertainty, and correct mistakes—so NRP reads as a serious desk, not a vibes feed.

Topic selection

  • We prioritize falsifiable questions where evidence exists (papers, official statistics, filings, reputable surveys).
  • We avoid personalized investment advice and single-stock hype framing.
  • If evidence is thin, we say so up front and narrow the claim.

Sourcing standards

  • Primary preferred: original paper, dataset documentation, regulator text, company filing.
  • Secondary allowed if clearly labeled; we still link upstream where possible.
  • We distinguish correlation vs. causation and model output vs. real-world outcome.
  • Images and charts built in-house label units, date range, and data source in caption or footnote.

AI tools

  • Assistive drafting may be used; every factual claim is verified by a human against listed sources before publish.
  • We do not pass off model hallucinations as citations—URLs and quotes are checked.

Corrections

Material errors are logged on the Corrections page and noted on the affected brief. Typographical fixes may be silent unless they change meaning.

Independence & conflicts

  • Sponsored or affiliate content, if ever used, will be disclosed prominently and will not use the Evidence Brief format.
  • Founders Divyam Gupta, Kaushik Atla, and Shivam Pande disclose relevant personal or family financial ties in internal notes; if a topic is conflicted, we skip or assign an independent reviewer.
