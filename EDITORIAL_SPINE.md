# Neural Report — editorial spine

**Co-founders:** Divyam Gupta (editorial lead), Kaushik Atla (research & web), Shivam Pande (design & partnerships). Role detail: [COFOUNDER_ROLES.md](COFOUNDER_ROLES.md).

## Primary spine (chosen)

**Path A — Mini-research desk**

We translate AI and economics for non-experts using **evidence-first briefs**, not hot takes.

## Weekly deliverable (named)

**NRP Evidence Brief** — one post per week (minimum), same structure every time, archived on the owned site (`docs/archive.html` + `docs/briefs/` in the GitHub repo).

### What “Evidence Brief” means

- Starts from a **specific question** (e.g., “Does AI adoption show up in firm-level productivity yet?”).
- States **2–4 falsifiable claims** the audience might believe after reading headlines.
- For each claim: **primary or high-quality secondary source**, quote or paraphrase with limits, and **what would change our mind**.
- Ends with **“Still uncertain because…”** (data gaps, conflicted literature, or policy in flux).

### Blended elements (secondary, optional)

- **Path C (journalism)** once per month: a short Q&A or expert pull-quote that feeds the next Evidence Brief.
- **Path B (data)** when we ship a chart: link to methodology and raw source table in `/archive/` or appendix.

## Non-goals

- Breaking news without primary sourcing.
- Price calls or personalized financial advice.
- Undisclosed sponsored framing.

## Instagram ↔ owned assets

- IG: teaser + one key figure + link in bio to full brief on site.
- Site: canonical URL, citations, corrections log.
